💎 Bijou AI: Official Brand Identity & Asset Directory (2026 Edition)
This guide establishes the visual and operational standards for Bijou AI, a production of W3J LLC. Use these specifications to maintain consistency across all Vercel-deployed landing pages, Supabase-powered dashboards, and Stripe-integrated checkout flows.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Visual Identity & Color Palette
Our palette is designed to project a "Premium Digital Employee" image—balancing high-tech indigo with growth-oriented emerald.
• Primary (Brand/Trust): #6366F1 (Indigo Blue). Used for action buttons, highlights, and primary CTA backgrounds.
• Secondary (Growth/Success): #10B981 (Emerald Green). Used for "Connected" statuses, profit indicators, and "Success" badges.
• Accent (Urgency/Attention): #F59E0B (Amber). Used for "Disconnected" alerts and high-priority escalation warnings.
• Background (Professional Dark): #0F172A (Slate 900). The primary background for the Client Dashboard to reduce eye strain for agents working late hours.
• Background (Clean Light): #FFFFFF (White). Used for the customer-facing pitch deck and public-facing marketing materials.
• Text (Readability): #1F2937 (Dark Gray). Standard body text color for light-mode documents.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Logo & Iconography
• The Mark: A geometric, metallic green diamond/gemstone icon.
• Styling: 3D rendered effect with sharp facets to represent precision and "The Royal Standard of AI".
• Usage:
    ◦ Full Logo: Placed on login and onboarding pages.
    ◦ Favicon: Simplified gem icon for browser tabs.
    ◦ Badge: "Powered by Bijou AI" footer on the Free Tier.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Typography & Text Styles
• Headings: Inter Bold or Outfit. Clean, modern, and high-impact.
• Body Text: Inter Regular. Optimized for readability at small sizes on mobile devices.
• Data/Metrics: Inter SemiBold. Used for ROI calculators and analytics dashboards to emphasize numbers.
• Dashboard Labels: Bilingual (EN/MY) labels for all key modules (Analytics, Calls, Contacts).
