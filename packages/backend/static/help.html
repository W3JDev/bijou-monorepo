🏠 Property Agents: Import Listings to Your AI

Train Bijou with your property listings so it can answer buyer and investor questions 24/7 on WhatsApp — automatically.

The fastest way — paste your PropertyGuru, iProperty, or EdgeProp listing link and Bijou AI will extract all property details automatically.

1
Go to Knowledge Base
In your dashboard, tap the Knowledge tab in the bottom navigation.
2
Click "🏠 Import Listing"
Select the Import Listing tab at the top of the Knowledge section.
3
Paste the Listing URL
Copy the full URL from PropertyGuru, iProperty, EdgeProp, Mudah, or any property site and paste it in the URL field.
4
Add an Agent Note (optional)
Add context like "This is our main project in PJ, focus on block B units" to help Bijou answer more accurately.
5
Click "Import Listing"
Bijou AI will scrape the page, extract property details, format them, and save to your knowledge base. Takes about 5–10 seconds.
6
Done! Bijou is now trained on this listing
The listing appears under Documents. Bijou will now answer buyer questions about price, size, facilities, and more.

Some property sites use JavaScript that prevents direct scraping. If the URL method fails, use paste mode instead.

1
Open the Listing Page
Visit the listing on PropertyGuru or your site in your browser.
2
Copy All the Text
Select all visible text on the page (Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C) — including title, price, size, description, facilities.
3
The Paste Box Appears Automatically
If the URL can't be scraped, Bijou shows a yellow warning and a paste text area automatically. Paste your copied text there.
4
Click "Save to AI"
Bijou AI formats the raw text into a structured knowledge article and saves it to your knowledge base.
