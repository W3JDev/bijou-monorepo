## Understanding Your AI Assistant

### 🧠 How Bijou AI Thinks

Bijou AI uses advanced natural language processing specifically trained for Malaysian businesses:

**Language Detection Flow:**
```
Customer Message → Language Analysis → Context Understanding → Response Generation
```

**Response Decision Tree:**
1. **Can I answer this confidently?** → Provide helpful response
2. **Is this too complex?** → Escalate to human
3. **Is customer frustrated?** → Switch to empathy mode & escalate
4. **Need appointment booking?** → Integrate with Google Calendar

### 🌍 Multi-Language Capabilities

**Supported Languages:**
- **English**: Standard business English with Malaysian context
- **Bahasa Malaysia**: Formal and casual Malay
- **Mandarin Chinese**: Simplified characters, business context
- **Tamil**: Basic customer service phrases
- **Manglish**: Mixed language detection and appropriate responses

**Example Conversations:**

**English Inquiry:**
```
Customer: "What are your operating hours?"
Bijou AI: "We're open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM, and Saturday 9 AM to 2 PM. We're closed on Sundays and public holidays. Is there anything specific you'd like to know about our services?"
```

**Manglish Inquiry:**
```
Customer: "Eh, your shop got delivery or not ah?"
Bijou AI: "Yes, we provide delivery service within Klang Valley! Delivery charge is RM8 for orders below RM50. Free delivery for orders above RM50 lah. You want to place order ah?"
```

**Bahasa Malaysia Inquiry:**
```
Customer: "Boleh book appointment tak?"
Bijou AI: "Boleh! Saya boleh tolong book appointment untuk anda. Bila anda nak datang? Saya akan check availability dan hantar calendar invite terus."
```

### 🎯 Smart Escalation Triggers

Bijou AI automatically escalates to humans when it detects:

**Frustration Indicators:**
- Repeated questions (customer not satisfied with AI response)
- Negative sentiment words ("angry", "disappointed", "terrible")
- Request for manager/supervisor
- Threats to leave bad review or complain

**Complexity Indicators:**
- Technical troubleshooting beyond basic FAQ
- Custom pricing negotiations
- Legal or compliance questions
- Refund/warranty claim processing

**Urgency Indicators:**
- Emergency keywords ("urgent", "asap", "emergency")
- Time-sensitive requests
- Payment-related issues
- Service disruption reports

## Troubleshooting & Support

### 🔧 Common Issues & Solutions

**Issue: AI Not Responding to Messages**
- ✅ Check WhatsApp Business connection status in dashboard
- ✅ Verify phone number format (include +60 country code)
- ✅ Ensure WhatsApp Business is active and not web-only
- ✅ Contact support if issue persists >5 minutes

**Issue: Wrong Language Detection**
- ✅ Check if customer is mixing multiple languages
- ✅ Enable Manglish mode if appropriate for your market
- ✅ Review language settings in AI configuration
- ✅ Provide feedback to improve detection accuracy

**Issue: Too Many Escalations**
- ✅ Review escalation triggers in settings
- ✅ Add more FAQs to AI knowledge base
- ✅ Train AI with industry-specific terminology
- ✅ Adjust sensitivity settings for your business type

**Issue: Low Customer Satisfaction**
- ✅ Monitor conversation logs for improvement areas
- ✅ Update AI personality to match brand voice
- ✅ Add more context about your business services
- ✅ Train staff on effective takeover procedures

## Advanced Training Scenarios

### 🎓 Role-Playing Exercises

**Scenario 1: Frustrated Customer**
```
Customer: "I've been calling for 3 days and nobody picks up! This is terrible service!"

Recommended AI Response: "I sincerely apologize for the frustration you've experienced. Missing your calls is absolutely not acceptable, and I understand how annoying this must be for you. 

Let me immediately connect you with our manager who can address this personally. While I arrange that, may I get your phone number so we can ensure this never happens again?"

Key Points: Acknowledge frustration, apologize sincerely, escalate immediately, offer resolution
```

**Scenario 2: Price-Sensitive Inquiry**
```
Customer: "Your competitor offers the same service for RM100 less"

Recommended AI Response: "I appreciate you sharing that information with me. Price is definitely an important consideration! 

What I'd love to do is have our business development manager discuss our value proposition with you personally - we often have package deals and value-adds that might not be immediately visible in basic pricing.

Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call where we can understand your specific needs and show you the complete picture?"

Key Points: Don't argue about price, focus on value, offer personal consultation
```
